### 3.9: DBaaS vs DIY

- DBaaS pro: It is easy to get things up and running, with provided automated processes, and the fact that no physical infrastructure is needed to be kept up and maintained can be a relief. It also requires no staff, extra work or systems to ensure all is ok, unlike with DIY.
- DBaaS con: If the chosen vendor is unsuitable, it may be difficult to change vendors later.
- DBaaS pro: They are usually secure from the get-go, whereas a DIY solution may need to have security explicitly implemented.
- DBaaS con: Although security often comes with DBaaS as a given, they usually have their own security protocols, and may be difficult to customize to one's own standards. Picking a vendor based on the provided security features may take time.
- DBaaS pro: Scalability is not an issue, unlike with a DIY solution where one may need to acquire more hardware or extra servers is some cases.
- DBaaS pro/con: Depending on one's needs, it may be too costly to use DBaaS services compared to DIY. Bigger companies may want to manage their own infrastructure, but smaller startups may benefit from using a DBaaS at the start.
- DBaaS con: If data is especially sensitive, it is best to ensure privacy using DIY solutions rather than storing data in a cloud via DBaaS.
- DBaaS con: DIY in general may offer more customization options for complex topologies etc.
- DBaaS pro: Backups are usually automated and easy to configure and manage. Due to the nature of DIY, this is something that one must implement explicitly.
